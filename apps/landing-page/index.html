01STATEMENT OF INTENT

Intelligence is a craft, not a trick..

I started with pixels. When I first watched a convolutional network learn to tell one face from the next, I stopped seeing vision as perception and started seeing it as a discipline — annotation, pre-processing, augmentation, patience. Everything I build now begins there.

The second lesson came from a hand. Building a real-time sign-language translator meant confronting the difference between a notebook accuracy of 92% and a classroom where the lighting is wrong, the hands are fast, and the frame rate fights you. Accuracy is necessary. Robustness is required.

I am drawn to work that sits just beyond the demo: deepfake detection that can be scrutinised, stroke risk models that clinicians could trust, assistants that remember context. I do not chase benchmarks for their own sake. I chase systems that survive contact with the real world.

This portfolio is arranged as a small magazine rather than a list — an index of work, a few convictions, and the record of a studio still learning. I am finishing a B.Tech in Computer Science (AI & ML), and I am, as the marginalia say, open to one project at a time.

Signed — Santhosh Sunkara, machine learning engineer & applied ai developer

03BRIEF BIOGRAPHY

Signed, sealed, still learning.

An engineer who treats model-building as a discipline of annotation, patience and honest evaluation.

I am an applied AI developer with a product instinct and an engineer's patience. My work lives in computer vision and language systems — real-time gesture recognition, deepfake detection, predictive health models — built to survive real environments rather than demos.

I interned with Google's AI-ML virtual programme and researched at the Edunet Foundation, where Sign Speak — my hand-sign system — was selected for the regional round. Between internships I served as an ML teaching assistant at KIET, guided two dozen students through regression to neural networks, and discovered that teaching is the fastest way to find the holes in what you know.

I hold B.Tech in Computer Science (AI & ML), write in English, Telugu and Hindi, and keep eighteen Google Cloud badges as a quiet shelf of evidence. I am, at heart, someone who believes the model is finished only when it works in the world.
